FAQ

Clear objection handling is another common pattern on the best pages.

Why wait 30 days for weekly service customers?

It gives the customer time to feel the value of consistent service first, which improves review quality and reduces complaints.

What does the $99 add-on cover?

It is a one time setup to connect the repairs and one time jobs trigger, so the review text goes out the following morning. Monthly stays $49.

Will this annoy my customers?

No. We send one request at a sensible time, and we track reviewers so nobody gets asked twice.
