FAQ

Do I need a call?

No. We can set it up from your intake form. Calls are optional if you want to talk through preferences.

Is this compliant with Google review policies?

We do this compliance-first: no incentives, and we avoid “review gating” language. We use a support-first message that works even if customers ignore images.

Will this spam my customers?

No. We cap frequency by default (e.g., max once per 90 days per customer) and include opt-out handling.

What do you need from me?

Company name, Google Business “leave a review” link, Skimmer access/login email, and which customers should receive requests.

What if I want to pause?
